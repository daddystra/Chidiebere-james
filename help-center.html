FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How long does delivery take?

Standard shipping usually takes 3–5 business days, while express shipping is available for quicker delivery depending on your location.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Yes. Unworn items in their original condition can be returned or exchanged within the return window stated on your order confirmation.

How do I track my order?

Once your item ships, you’ll receive a confirmation email and tracking link so you can follow it all the way to your door.

What payment options are available?

We support secure online payment options for guest and registered shoppers, with checkout designed to be quick and safe.
